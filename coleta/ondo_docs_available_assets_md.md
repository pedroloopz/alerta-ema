# Available Assets

<div id="geofenced-content">
  ### What assets are available to trade through Ondo Stocks?

  Investors can currently trade 100+ tokenized stocks and ETFs via the Ondo Stocks platform, including the stock of popular companies (e.g. Tesla, Nvidia, Figma), indexes (e.g. QQQ, SPY), and fixed income ETFs (e.g. TLT, TIP, AGG). For a complete list, please visit our web app at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance).

  ### Do you just offer tokenized stocks? What about fixed income? ETFs? Mutual Funds?

  Yes, ETFs are available—when we say 'tokenized stocks' we intend to refer to the full set of available assets.

  ### What other stocks and ETFs do you intend to tokenize in the future?

  The Ondo Stocks platform was designed to be scalable quickly, and we intend to tokenize thousands of publicly traded assets over the coming months. Stay tuned.

  ### Do you only offer US stocks?

  We currently only offer tokenized versions of securities trading on the NYSE and NASDAQ exchanges. Over time, however, we may expand to tokenizing securities trading on exchanges in other countries.

  ### What is USDon?

  USDon is a stablecoin backed 1:1 by a US dollar in one of Ondo Stocks' brokerage accounts. USDon is the stablecoin used to instantly buy or redeem Ondo tokenized stocks.  When you purchase Ondo tokenized stocks with other stablecoins such as USDC, our platform [atomically swaps that stablecoin for USDon](/ondo-stocks/investing-and-redeeming#what-forms-of-payment-can-i-use-to-purchase-ondo-tokenized-stocks) and then uses USDon to buy the tokenized stock. Similarly, when you sell your tokenized stock for USDC, our platform first redeems for USDon and then swaps that for USDC, all in one atomic transaction.
